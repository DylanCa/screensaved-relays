Screensaved

A screensaver for Apple TV: beautiful wallpapers with glanceable widgets in the corners of your screen.

Widgets
  • Clock — world clocks across timezones
  • Weather — current conditions, forecast, air quality
  • Music — now playing from Spotify or Apple Music, with remote controls
  • Calendar — your upcoming events from iCloud and Google Calendar
  • Releases — upcoming episodes, movies and games from your watchlist
  • Steam — your gaming activity, achievements and friends
  • Daily — quote of the day, word of the day, "on this day"
Wallpapers
  • Curated gradients
  • Aerial videos (provided by Pexels)
  • Your own photos via iCloud Shared Albums

Everything runs on your Apple TV — no account with us, no backend, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
