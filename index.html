Screensaved

A screensaver app for Apple TV.

What is Screensaved?

The purpose of Screensaved is to turn an idle Apple TV into a useful, beautiful display. While your TV is not in use, Screensaved shows a full-screen wallpaper (curated gradients, aerial videos, or your own photos) with small, glanceable information widgets in the corners of the screen — so you can see the time, the weather, your agenda, or what's playing at a glance, from your couch.

Widgets

  • Clock — current time and world clocks across timezones
  • Weather — current conditions, 3-day forecast, air quality
  • Calendar — your upcoming events from Google Calendar and iCloud
  • Music — now playing from Spotify or Apple Music, with remote controls
  • Releases — upcoming episodes, movies and games from your watchlist
  • Steam — your gaming activity, achievements and friends
  • Daily — quote of the day, word of the day, "on this day"

Google Calendar integration

If you connect your Google account, Screensaved uses read-only access to your calendars (calendar.readonly) for a single purpose: showing the titles and start times of your upcoming events in the Calendar widget on your own television. Events are fetched directly from the Google Calendar API to your Apple TV, are never stored beyond a short in-memory cache, and are never sent anywhere else. Screensaved has no backend server. You can disconnect your Google account at any time in Settings, which deletes the stored sign-in token.

Privacy

Screensaved has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. All data stays between your Apple TV and the services you choose to connect. Read the full privacy policy.
