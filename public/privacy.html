Screensaved — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Screensaved is a screensaver app for Apple TV. It has no backend server, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We (the developer) never see, collect, store, or sell any of your data.

Where your data goes

Everything the app knows lives on your Apple TV. When you enable a widget, the app talks directly from your Apple TV to the service that powers it — never through any server of ours:

  • Weather — your location (or a city you type) is sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast.
  • Music — with Spotify, the app uses your own Spotify application credentials and talks to Spotify's API on your behalf. With Apple Music, playback data stays within Apple's frameworks.
  • Calendar — your iCloud (app-specific password) or Google Calendar credentials are used to fetch events directly from Apple or Google.
  • Releases — your SIMKL watchlist is fetched from SIMKL; upcoming games come from IGDB.
  • Steam — your public Steam profile data is fetched from the Steam Web API.
  • Wallpapers — videos and photos are fetched from Pexels or from your iCloud Shared Album's public link.

Credentials and tokens

Sign-in tokens (Spotify, SIMKL, Google, iCloud) are stored only in your Apple TV's keychain and preferences. They never leave the device except to authenticate directly with the service they belong to. Disconnecting an account in Settings deletes its token.

Location

If you allow location access, it is used only to fetch local weather from Open-Meteo. You can instead type a city name, in which case the device's location is never accessed.

Third-party services

Each service you choose to connect has its own privacy policy: Open-Meteo, Spotify, Apple, Google, SIMKL, IGDB, Steam, Pexels, ZenQuotes.

Children

Screensaved does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
